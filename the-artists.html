The Artists


Sphere partners with legendary icons to create the unprecedented. Here, visionaries push the boundaries of what’s possible, crafting moments of wonder that captivate and inspire.

Anyma

Anyma – A Visionary in Electronic Music


Anyma (Matteo Milleri) is a globally acclaimed artist known for blending melodic techno with immersive audiovisual experiences. His debut albums Genesys and Genesys II (Afterlife/Interscope) explore technology, nature, and humanity, featuring collaborations with Grimes and Sevdaliza. As one half of Tale Of Us and co-founder of Afterlife Records, Anyma’s viral live performances push artistic boundaries with cutting-edge visuals and sound design. His remixes of Fred Again… and Lana Del Rey have gained massive success, and he will headline Ultra 2025 with Solomun for a world-exclusive B2B set.

Darren Aronofsky

Postcard From Earth – Now Live


Academy Award®-nominated filmmaker Darren Aronofsky, born in Brooklyn, is known for visionary films like *Black Swan*, *Requiem for a Dream*, and *The Whale*. His work spans from psychological thrillers (*mother!*) to epic dramas (*Noah*). As a producer, he has backed acclaimed films like *Jackie* and documentaries such as *The Territory*. Aronofsky also explores the natural world through projects like *Welcome to Earth* and *Limitless*. A passionate environmental advocate, he serves on the boards of The Sierra Club and The School for Field Studies.

Dead & Company

Dead & Company – A Touring Legacy


Dead & Company became one of the most successful touring bands, drawing nearly five million fans across 300 shows. Their 30-show residency at Sphere in Las Vegas attracted nearly half a million attendees. They set records at Wrigley Field for single-concert attendance, Folsom Field (13 shows), and Citi Field (11 shows), while also breaking Fenway Park’s all-time ticket sales record. The band closed their touring career with three final shows at Oracle Park, playing to over 118,000 fans.

Eagles

Eagles – Timeless Icons of American Music


Formed in 1971, the Eagles pioneered a blend of country, folk, R&B, rock, and pop, becoming one of the most successful bands of all time. With over 150 million albums sold, six #1 albums, and six GRAMMYs, they were inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 1998. Their Greatest Hits 1971-1977 remains the best-selling album in history (38× Platinum), while Hotel California (26× Platinum) is the third best-selling U.S. album ever.

U2

U2 – Pushing Boundaries for Over Four Decades


Formed in Dublin in 1978, U2 has sold over 170 million albums, won 22 Grammys, and was inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2005. With 15 studio albums, their chart-topping success spans four decades. Known for groundbreaking tours like The Joshua Tree Tour and eXPERIENCE + iNNOCENCE, U2 continues to redefine live performances. In 2023, Songs of Surrender topped global charts, and U2:UV Achtung Baby Live At Sphere made history, drawing 700,000 fans across 40 sold-out shows.
