Anyma’s Final Residency: A Retrospective

May 25, 2024

Dive into the groundbreaking visuals and sound design of Anyma’s final residency at The Sphere. This residency redefined live electronic music with its innovative use of holographic stage effects and spatial audio technology.

From the opening night to the grand finale, each performance was a unique journey through sound and light. Fans were treated to a mix of classic hits and new tracks, all enhanced by Sphere’s cutting-edge technology.
