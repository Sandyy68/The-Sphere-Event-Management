Dead & Company: Dead Forever

June 20, 2024

Join us on a deep dive into the mesmerizing experience of Dead & Company's "Dead Forever" residency at The Sphere. This spectacular event blended the spirit of The Grateful Dead with modern technological wonders, creating an unforgettable tribute to their legendary music.

Featuring stunning psychedelic visuals synchronized to the music, The Sphere's immersive LED display transported fans into an electrifying journey through time and sound. Every performance felt like a spiritual revival, with classic songs reimagined through mind-bending effects and a 360-degree audiovisual experience.

Beyond the visuals, the spatial audio system ensured that every note and lyric resonated with pristine clarity, embracing fans in a surround-sound experience unlike any other. "Dead Forever" was not just a concert—it was an odyssey through decades of musical heritage and innovation.
