UON Visuals

3D Artist


UON Visuals is a 3D artist based in Vancouver, BC, known for pushing the boundaries of technology to create ultra-vivid, mind-bending visual experiences. Using math formulas and code, he crafts intricate animated patterns and dynamic color spectrums that engage the senses in ways beyond ordinary perception. Far from minimalist, his art is a stimulating overload of complex visuals—captivating, intense, and filled with mathematical beauty. As technology evolves, so does his work. His goal is to harness new technologies to their fullest, creating unparalleled visual experiences that break conventional boundaries.

Sphere Artwork Titles:

CircuitTubeVR, HexFormulaStacks, StarWavePortal, VoxelCubesSpirals, DMTorus, HypnoTunnelTwirls, SpectralBloops

Sphere Debut: December 31, 2024

POST WOOK

Collage Artist


Natasha Chomko is a Los Angeles based digital artist mainly focused on surreal landscape collage art with refined visionary and psychedelic elements. Her style aims to be palatable for both the seasoned psychonaut and average Joe, and everyone in between. Her work combines snippets of landscapes from all over the globe to demonstrate how interconnected we all truly are. As a lifelong collager, Natasha challenges perception of shapes, color, and texture to create entirely new universes out of pre-existing photos.   

Sphere Artwork Title:

Everywhere but Inward

Sphere Debut: December 13, 2024

RISTAN EATON

CFine Artist & Muralist


Tristan Eaton is perhaps best known for his large scale public murals, found throughout the world from New York to Paris to Shanghai, which he executes in freehand spray paint; a technical and personal nod to his own history and respect for graffiti culture, while honoring traditional painting and muralism with his thoughtful and dynamic subject matter and compositions. All of these experiences become touchstones in Eaton's fine art practice where techniques new and old meet unusual and innovative ideas paired with an assortment of methods and materials both traditional and modern, all of which find a home and harmony in his work.

Sphere Artwork Title:

Gemma

Sphere Debut: November 15, 2024

JAMES TRALIE

3D Artist


James Tralie is a planetary science producer, animator, and TV host at NASA. He works at the intersection of science and art to create dreamy, otherworldly scenes, education explainer videos, documentary productions, and immersive content. 

Sphere Artwork Title:

Planetarium Gardens

Sphere Debut: October 12, 2024

AEFORIA

Digital Artist


Aeforia is an artist from Montreal, Canada, renowned for his captivating and emotive artwork. The art of Aeforia revolves around the creation of ethereal characters, brought to life with the help of digital fashion design and detailed organic sculpting as forms of expression. Each piece is a reflection of his introspection, translating his own human experiences into otherworldly figures and surreal landscapes. His visual explorations showcase an expressive yet sophisticated use of light, texture, color, and composition. They are an invitation to escape for a moment and dive into an alternative universe, rich in poetic contrasts.

Sphere Artwork Title:

L’Élue

Sphere Debut: September 20, 2024

JOSUE IBANEZ

Media Artist


Josue Ibañez is a media artist from Oaxaca, Mexico. He is a visionary in the multimedia industry with a career spanning almost two decades. His work has explored the possibilities of modern storytelling to provoke emotions through digital and physical experiences. Josue's work revolves around creating multimedia environments and immersive installations, driven by his passion for uncovering his fascination with technology and aesthetics
